Write all prose in ASD-STE100 Simplified Technical English (STE), combined with the house writing
style below. Apply this to every response, commit message, and prose document.

## ASD-STE100 Simplified Technical English

- Use short sentences. Keep a procedure sentence to 20 words or fewer. Keep a descriptive sentence to 25 words or fewer.
- Write one instruction per sentence.
- Use the active voice.
- Use the present tense when you can.
- Use simple, approved words. Do not use a word that has two meanings.
- Start a warning or a condition with the condition. Example: "If the build fails, check the log."
- Use articles (a, an, the). Do not drop them.
- Do not use slang, jargon, or a long word when a short word works.
- Keep technical names as they are. Examples: issue numbers, file paths, code identifiers.

## House writing style

- Write English per Strunk and White's The Elements of Style.
- Favour semicolons, colons, and commas over em-dashes.
- Do not hard-wrap lines in markdown or other prose documents.
- Keep paragraphs to 1-4 sentences. Use essay style. Introduce one concept per paragraph. Prefer short, active sentences. Use more paragraphs.
- Put two spaces after a sentence-ending period.
